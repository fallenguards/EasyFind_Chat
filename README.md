# EasyFind: Chat Extension

A messenger for WoW's chat. Whispers, Battle.net friends, party, raid, guild, Say and your channels each keep a conversation of their own, with its history, in one window with a list of chats beside it.

## Requirements

- EasyFind

Retail, WoW Forever, Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and Mists Classic.

## Features

- A conversation for every whisper, Battle.net friend, group and channel, kept between sessions.
- Message bubbles with times, names in their class colors, and avatars you can change.
- Reply to a message or forward it; pin a message and jump back to it later.
- A New messages line where your unread messages start, and a button back to the newest.
- Search every chat, filter the open one with Ctrl+F, or show one person's messages with from:Name.
- Folders, pinned and muted chats, an unread count on the chat button, and messages that name you standing out.
- Unsent drafts kept per chat; away and busy replies, and whispers that never arrived, shown in the chat.
- Web addresses copy on click; item links and EasyFind links work inside the bubbles.
- Optional: use it as the game's chat, with slash commands and the Combat Log inside its window.

## How to open it

Click Chat in EasyFind's extensions menu, or type chat in EasyFind's search bar and pick the Chat row. Its settings are in EasyFind's options, under Extensions > Chat.
